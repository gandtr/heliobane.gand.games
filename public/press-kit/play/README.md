# Google Play

`feature_1024x500.png`: the feature graphic of HELIOBANE on Google Play
(play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=games.gand.heliobane), 1024×500, for Play Console →
Grow → Store presence → Main store listing → Graphics → Feature graphic.

The link-preview composition (as the Steam main capsule and the Epic landscape image): the
sun-eye painting with the eye right of centre, the logo centred across the top, and HB-0 at
the bottom centre firing the Breaker straight up into the logo's O. Drawn on a 512×250 native
canvas in the game's 32 colours, then ×2 nearest-neighbour. Play crops nothing from the
feature graphic but may lay its own controls along the bottom, so the logo stays in the top
half.
