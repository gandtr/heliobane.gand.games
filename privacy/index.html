Privacy policy

HELIOBANE by Gand Games · Effective 31 October 2026

HELIOBANE does not collect, keep or use your personal data. Your saves stay on your device. The only data that leaves it goes to the store you got the game from, so you can earn achievements and, on phones, unlock the full game. Gand never receives it.

This policy covers HELIOBANE on Steam, the Epic Games Store and Google Play, and the Amiga and X68000 versions on itch.io, which run fully offline. HELIOBANE is made and published by Gand (Gand Games), an independent studio based in Tokyo, Japan.

What data is captured, and how

  • Saves and settings: campaign progress, high scores (initials you type yourself) and options, written to files on your device.
  • Your store account, for achievements: the game signs in to the store you play on so it can read and unlock your achievements. On the Epic Games Store, the full game asks only for the Basic Profile permission (Epic account ID, display name, language and linked-account display names) and uses only your Epic account ID and an Epic Online Services player ID.
  • Purchase status (phones): the game asks the store whether you bought the full game.
  • Motion sensor (phones): tilt steering uses the readings on the spot. They are never saved or sent.

How the data is used

Only to run the game: saves let you continue, your store account unlocks your achievements, and the purchase status unlocks the full game. There are no ads, analytics or tracking.

Who the data is shared with

Only the store you play on: Epic Games, Valve (Steam, including Steam Cloud saves for the full game) or Google (Google Play and Play Games). Each handles it under its own privacy policy: Epic Games, Steam and Google. Gand receives nothing from the game, only the anonymous sales reports and crash reports the stores give every developer.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

Files on your device stay until you delete them. On desktop they are in Gand/Heliobane (demo: Gand/Heliobane-Demo) under %APPDATA% on Windows, ~/Library/Application Support on macOS and ~/.local/share on Linux and Steam Deck. On phones, uninstalling the game deletes them. Your achievements and purchase belong to your store account and are kept by that store. You can remove HELIOBANE's access to your Epic account at any time in your Epic account settings.

Permissions (Android)

Internet, used only by Google Play to buy the full game and unlock achievements, and Vibrate, for haptic feedback you can turn off in the options.

Children

HELIOBANE is not directed at children under 13. Store account services are subject to their providers' privacy policies and account requirements.

Changes

If the game changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
