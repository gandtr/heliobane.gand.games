Privacy policy

HELIOBANE by Gand Games · Effective 31 October 2026

Saves and settings stay on your device. HELIOBANE has no ads, analytics or tracking, and the game does not send data to any server run by Gand. The only data that leaves your device goes to the store you got the game from, for achievements, purchases and (on Steam) cloud saves, as described below.

This policy covers HELIOBANE on every platform: the phone versions on Google Play and the App Store, the desktop versions on Steam and the Epic Games Store, the Amiga and X68000 versions on itch.io, and this website. HELIOBANE is made and published by Gand (Gand Games), an independent studio based in Tokyo, Japan.

What data is captured, and how

  • Saves and settings (every version): your campaign progress, high-score table (three-letter initials you type yourself), options, language and control settings. The game writes them to files on your own device as you play.
  • Achievement progress: which achievements you have earned. The phone versions also keep the list in a file on the phone until the store has received it.
  • Epic account (Epic Games Store full game only): when the Epic Games Launcher starts the game, it hands the game your Epic account ID and a one-time sign-in code. The game uses the code to sign in with the Basic Profile permission, which covers your Epic account ID, display name, language and linked-account display names, and receives an Epic Online Services player ID. It saves your Epic account ID and any achievement unlocks that have not gone through yet in a file on your device. Epic's software development kit keeps its own cache files in a folder next to the saves. The game does not ask for friends, presence or email permissions. The Epic desktop demo does not use Epic sign-in or achievements.
  • Steam (Steam versions): the game asks Steam for your Steam language, to pick the game's language the first time, and for the type of controller connected, to show matching button prompts. The language becomes your starting language setting; the controller type is not saved.
  • Purchase status (phone versions): the game asks the store on your device whether the full game has been bought and keeps a copy of the answer on the device, so it doesn't have to ask every time.
  • Motion sensor (phone versions): tilt steering reads the device's accelerometer and gyroscope while you play. The readings are used on the spot to move your ship and are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Technical log (desktop versions): the game engine writes a log of its own messages from the last few runs (start-up details, warnings and errors) to a logs folder next to the saves.
  • Email: if you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you put in the message.
  • This website: it sets no cookies and runs no analytics or third-party scripts. If you switch the page language, your browser remembers the choice in its local storage on your device.

HELIOBANE does not collect your name, email address, payment details, location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers.

How the data is used

  • Saves and settings let you continue your campaign, see your high scores and keep your options.
  • Achievement progress is used to unlock achievements on your store account.
  • Your Epic account ID and Epic Online Services player ID are used only to read and unlock your Epic achievements. The saved account ID lets an unlock that failed be retried later for the same Epic account, and never for a different one.
  • Purchase status unlocks the full game on phones, including on a new phone or after a reinstall.
  • The technical log stays on your device. It only reaches us if you choose to send it with a bug report, and then we use it to find and fix the bug.
  • Emails are used only to answer you.

We do not use any of this for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

Who the data is shared with

The game sends data only to the store services listed here, and only for the version you play. Gand's own servers receive nothing from the game.

  • Epic Games (Epic Games Store full game): the one-time sign-in code, requests to read your achievements, and your achievement unlocks, tied to your Epic account ID and Epic Online Services player ID. The game does not upload your campaign save.
  • Valve (Steam versions): your achievement unlocks. For the full game, Steam Cloud also copies your save and settings files to your Steam account so they follow you to other computers. The technical log is not synced.
  • Google (Android): Google Play's billing service, to buy and restore the full game, and Google Play Games, to unlock achievements when you are signed in.
  • Apple (iPhone): the App Store, to buy and restore the full game, and Game Center, to unlock achievements when you are signed in.
  • The Amiga and X68000 versions do not connect to any network.
  • GitHub hosts this website (GitHub Pages). Like any web host, it handles the technical details of your visit, such as your IP address, to deliver the page; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Each of these services handles what it receives under its own privacy policy: Epic Games, Steam, Google and Apple. Payment is handled entirely by the store; we never see your name, email address or payment details.

What Gand receives: emails you send us; the anonymous, aggregate sales reports and statistics that the stores give every developer (for example, the number of sales per country); and any crash reports a store passes on, which contain technical data about the crash only. We do not share any of it with anyone else.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

The files on your device stay there until you delete them. Uninstalling the desktop game may leave them in place, so to remove everything, delete the game's data folder:

  • Windows: %APPDATA%\Gand\Heliobane
  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Gand/Heliobane
  • Linux and Steam Deck: ~/.local/share/Gand/Heliobane

The demo uses a Heliobane-Demo folder in the same place. Deleting the folder removes your saves, settings, high scores, saved Epic account ID, pending unlocks, Epic cache files and the technical log.

On phones, everything the game saves sits in the app's own storage. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes it. Your purchase stays with your Google or Apple account and can be restored.

Data held by a store (achievements, Steam Cloud copies of your saves, your purchase) is kept with your account on that store under its privacy policy, and deleting it is done through that store. You can also remove HELIOBANE's access to your Epic account at any time in your Epic account settings.

We keep emails only as long as we need them to answer you. To have yours deleted, write to the address below. The sales reports we receive contain no personal data.

On this website, the remembered language choice stays in your browser until you clear the site's data.

Permissions (Android)

  • Internet: used only by Google Play's billing service, to buy and restore the full game, and by Google Play Games, for achievements.
  • Vibrate: the phone versions can vibrate on hits and explosions. You can turn it off in the options.

Children

HELIOBANE is not directed at children under 13. Store account services are subject to their providers' privacy policies and account requirements.

Changes
